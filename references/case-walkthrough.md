# Case walkthrough: “Носи автора”

The supplied Clickmedia case is an applied example of the workshop flow for a B2C brand of limited-edition hoodies featuring contemporary Korean artists. It is useful for seeing the intended depth and formatting of Prompt 1 through Prompt 4; it does not establish facts for any other product.

## What the case demonstrates

1. **Prompt 1:** segment by a shared bundle of Core Jobs, Big Job, context, role, and criteria — rather than demographics alone. The example ranks five segments and provides TAM/SAM/SOM logic for each.
2. **Prompt 2:** reuse the selected segment’s Core Jobs and Big Job to build a Value Proposition Canvas. Each Pain Reliever and Gain Creator must connect to a named Pain or Gain; unmatched needs remain explicit gaps.
3. **Prompt 3:** translate the chosen offer into the ordered landing-page sections: oneliner, jobs and micro-jobs, an early “aha” moment, value, recognition, delivery, Point B, barriers, and alternatives.
4. **Prompt 4:** turn the approved copy into a responsive one-file HTML landing page.

## How to use the case safely

- Use it as a depth and output-shape example, never as market research for a different brand.
- Do not reuse its audience, artist claims, price assumptions, or TAM/SAM/SOM estimates.
- Keep the source’s distinction intact: a segment is a group with similar jobs and success criteria, not merely an age bracket or role.
